    The land is generated at random each time. Roads can cross water, at six times the price.

    How to play

    • Drag to build roads. Everything you build costs money.
    • Houses appear by themselves beside roads while residents are content.
    • Residents pay taxes. Raise the rate for more money — too high and they move out.
    • Shops give people jobs and somewhere to drive. Parks lift the mood.
    • Toll gates earn money from every passing car, but slow traffic and annoy residents.
    • Lights, roundabouts and bridges unlock as the town grows.
    • Right-drag or the bulldozer demolishes. Space pauses. The letter on each tool selects it.
