Controls

  • Click a tile to place a road.
  • Drag to lay a connected stretch of road.
  • Roads join their neighbours on their own — cross two roads to make an intersection.
  • Pick the Bulldozer, or right-drag, to remove roads.
  • Press R or B to switch tools.
